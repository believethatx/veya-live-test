# Gift artwork and animation credits

The 25 Veya test gift animations are Google's Animated Noto Emoji, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/. The still SVG thumbnails were rendered from the animations' marked rest frames and resized in Veya's interface. Individual animation JSON was obtained from https://github.com/quarrel/noto-emoji-dotlottie at commit 1982a36bb8fb0a3b8791fccc815d91fb72f3e3901.

The local web renderer, `public/lottie-light.min.js`, is [lottie-web](https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-web) from commit bede03d25d232826e0c9dca1733d542d8a7754fb2. Its MIT license follows.
